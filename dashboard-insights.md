# Sales Analytics Dashboard: Key Insights

Source: Simple Store "Sales Analytics" Power BI report (Sales Overview, Sales vs. Target and Product Analysis). The data is fictional and covers 2020–2022. M means million; values read from charts are approximate.

## Page 1: Sales Overview
- **Overall performance:** Total sales are **4,048M** with **2,221M** gross margin, about **55% of sales**, across 3 store types, 5 departments and 45 products.
- **Store types:** CORE generates about **3,000M (74%)**, DIGITAL about 780M (19%) and LOCAL about 270M (7%). Sales depend heavily on CORE stores.
- **Different mix in LOCAL stores:** Clothing is the top department for CORE and DIGITAL, but LOCAL stores sell more Kitchen and Other than Clothing. LOCAL stores also cluster in the southeastern states on the map, so a regional or format effect is worth checking.
- **Seasonality:** November is the peak, at about **490M** across the three years, roughly 45% above the average month (about 337M). March is second at about 385M, and December is the lowest month at about 280M, so the November peak is short and sharp.
- **Flat annual sales:** Sales were about 1.33B in 2020 and 1.33B in 2021, then grew **4.2%** to **1.39B** in 2022.

## Page 2: Sales vs. Target

- **Target achievement:** Sales of **4,048M** beat the **3,757M** target, reaching about **108% of target**, a surplus of 292M.
- **Where the surplus comes from:** CORE accounts for most of it, and DIGITAL and LOCAL are only slightly above target.
- **Current-year KPIs:** This year's sales (2022, the latest year in the data) are **1,387M**, up **4.17%** on the prior year (1,332M) and **10.01%** above the 1,261M target.
- **Trend:** The quarterly chart shows sales below target in all four quarters of 2020, close to target in 2021 and above target in all four quarters of 2022.
- **Stores below target:** Among the visible states, **West Virginia reached 94% (13M short)**, **Oklahoma 96% (5M short)** and **Nevada 98% (2M short)**.
- **West Virginia's target looks demanding:** Its target of 212M is the highest on screen, above Texas at 208M, while its sales of 199M rank second. Check whether the target is realistic before treating it as underperformance.
- **Strongest results:** Connecticut (125%), Pennsylvania (123%) and Iowa (119%) beat their targets by the widest margins. Several stores only just beat target, such as Texas (104%), Wisconsin (103%) and Maryland (101%), leaving little buffer.

## Page 3: Product Analysis

- **Department contribution:** Clothing is the largest department at about **1,350M, one-third of total sales**. Garage is next at 784M, about 19%.
- **Top products by sales:** Kitchens lead at about **400M**, followed by Garden Appliances (about 300M), Womens (about 293M), Accessories (about 265M) and Bicycle Storage (about 207M).
- **Margin differs from sales rank:** Kitchens also lead on gross margin (about 230M). Womens ranks third on sales but second on gross margin (about 160M), ahead of Garden Appliances.
- **Growth within departments:** Within Clothing, Womens (293M) and Accessories (203M) lead, while Infants is the smallest (29M) and the slowest growing. Electric Doors (Garage) is the fastest-growing visible product, but from a small base (54M).

## Recommendations

- **Prepare for peak demand:** Review November and March demand and prioritise stock and staffing for Clothing and Kitchens.
- **Investigate local shortfalls:** Examine West Virginia, Oklahoma and Nevada to see whether the gap relates to demand, stock or target assumptions. Start with the West Virginia target.
- **Look beyond sales rank:** Kitchens lead on both sales and gross margin, and Womens ranks higher on margin than on sales, so prioritise availability for these two alongside Clothing overall.
- **Plan by store type:** Use CORE and Clothing as the base for planning, but plan LOCAL stores around their Kitchen and Other mix.

## Interpretation Notes

- The state table scrolls, so the stores below target may not be a complete list.
- The status icons in the state table show sales percentile tiers (50th, 75th and 100th percentile of total sales). Target performance is shown in the **% of Target** and **Difference from Target** columns.
- With all years selected, % Sales Growth (52%) compares three years of sales (4,048M) with only two years of prior-year sales (2,661M), so it reads higher than any single year's growth. For year-on-year growth, see the KPI cards: +4.17% in 2022 (1,387M vs. 1,332M) and about 0% in 2021. Product growth rates are used here to compare products with each other.
- The report date (10 Nov 2026) is separate from the 2020–2022 sales period.
- These findings describe patterns in fictional data. Pricing, promotion, inventory and customer data would be needed to explain the causes.
